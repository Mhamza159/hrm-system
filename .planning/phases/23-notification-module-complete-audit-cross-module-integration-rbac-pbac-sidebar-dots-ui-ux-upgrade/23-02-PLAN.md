# Plan 23-02: Cross-Module Notification Trigger Instrumentation (16 Domains)

## Goal
Audit and instrument missing business workflow events across all HRMS domains to ensure notifications are dispatched to the appropriate recipients with correct module tags, priority levels, direct action links, and strict confidentiality enforcement.

## Proposed Changes

### 1. Recruitment & ATS (`backend/src/modules/recruitment/`)
- In `job.service.js` / `candidate.service.js`:
  - When a new candidate applies or is submitted: Notify assigned hiring managers / HR (`module: 'recruitment'`, `priority: 'normal'`, `link: '/recruitment/pipeline'`).
  - When interview is scheduled/rescheduled: Notify candidate and interviewers (`module: 'recruitment'`, `priority: 'high'`).
  - When candidate is offered: Notify candidate and HR Admin (`module: 'recruitment'`, `priority: 'high'`).

### 2. Employees & Lifecycle (`backend/src/modules/onboarding/`, `resignation.service.js`)
- In `resignation.service.js`:
  - When resignation is submitted: Notify direct reporting manager and HR (`module: 'lifecycle'`, `priority: 'high'`, `link: '/offboarding'`).
  - When exit clearance sign-off is approved: Notify employee and HR.
- In `onboarding.service.js`:
  - When lifecycle task is assigned: Notify the assigned employee (`module: 'lifecycle'`, `priority: 'normal'`, `link: '/onboarding/my-tasks'`).

### 3. Attendance & Shifts (`backend/src/modules/attendance/`)
- In `attendance.service.js`:
  - When missed punch correction request is submitted: Notify direct manager (`module: 'attendance'`, `priority: 'normal'`, `link: '/attendance/team'`).
  - When attendance is locked by HR: Dispatch company-wide notice (`module: 'attendance'`, `priority: 'low'`, `link: '/attendance/my'`).
  - When punch is flagged (geo-fence or mock GPS): Notify HR (`module: 'attendance'`, `priority: 'high'`, `link: '/attendance/flagged'`).

### 4. Leaves & Holidays (`backend/src/modules/leaves/`)
- In `leave.service.js`:
  - When leave is applied: Notify manager with direct approval link (`module: 'leaves'`, `priority: 'normal'`, `link: '/leaves/approvals'`).
  - When leave is approved or rejected: Notify employee with decision rationale (`module: 'leaves'`, `priority: 'high'`, `link: '/leaves'`).
  - When leave encashment request is submitted: Notify HR manager (`module: 'leaves'`, `priority: 'normal'`, `link: '/leaves/encashment'`).

### 5. Payroll (`backend/src/modules/payroll/`)
- In `payroll.service.js`:
  - When payroll batch run is completed: Notify Super Admin and HR (`module: 'payroll'`, `priority: 'normal'`, `link: '/payroll/runs'`).
  - When payslips are finalized/paid: Notify respective employees securely (`module: 'payroll'`, `priority: 'high'`, `link: '/payroll/my-payslips'`). Never expose salary numbers in plaintext notification body.
  - When salary revision is approved: Notify employee (`module: 'payroll'`, `priority: 'high'`, `link: '/me'`).

### 6. Loans & Advances (`backend/src/modules/loans/`)
- In `loan.service.js`:
  - When loan is applied: Notify designated approvers (`module: 'loans'`, `priority: 'normal'`, `link: '/loans/approvals'`).
  - When loan is approved/disbursed: Notify employee (`module: 'loans'`, `priority: 'high'`, `link: '/loans'`).

### 7. Expenses (`backend/src/modules/expenses/`)
- In `expense.service.js`:
  - When claim is submitted: Notify manager (`module: 'expenses'`, `priority: 'normal'`, `link: '/expenses/approvals'`).
  - When claim is approved/reimbursed: Notify employee (`module: 'expenses'`, `priority: 'normal'`, `link: '/expenses'`).

### 8. Assets & Inventory (`backend/src/modules/assets/`)
- In `asset.service.js`:
  - When asset is assigned to employee: Notify employee (`module: 'assets'`, `priority: 'high'`, `link: '/assets/my'`).
  - When asset requisition is approved/rejected: Notify employee (`module: 'assets'`, `priority: 'normal'`, `link: '/assets/requests'`).

### 9. Projects & Timesheets (`backend/src/modules/timesheets/`)
- In `timesheet.service.js`:
  - When weekly timesheet is submitted: Notify project manager (`module: 'projects'`, `priority: 'normal'`, `link: '/timesheets/approvals'`).
  - When overdue timesheet warning is triggered by cron: Notify delinquent employee (`module: 'projects'`, `priority: 'high'`, `link: '/timesheets/my'`).

### 10. Performance Management (`backend/src/modules/performance/`)
- In `performance.service.js`:
  - When appraisal cycle is launched: Notify all targeted employees (`module: 'performance'`, `priority: 'normal'`, `link: '/performance/my-review'`).
  - When review is submitted: Notify employee or manager (`module: 'performance'`, `priority: 'normal'`, `link: '/performance/team-reviews'`).

### 11. Company Policies (`backend/src/modules/policies/`)
- In `policy.service.js`:
  - When mandatory policy is published: Notify targeted audience (`module: 'policies'`, `priority: 'urgent'`, `link: '/policies'`).
  - When acknowledgement deadline is near: Trigger high priority reminder.

### 12. Announcements (`backend/src/modules/announcements/`)
- In `announcement.service.js`:
  - When 'urgent' announcement is created: Dispatch urgent notification to target audience (`module: 'announcements'`, `priority: 'urgent'`, `link: '/announcements'`).

### 13. Rewards & Kudos (`backend/src/modules/rewards/`)
- In `reward.service.js`:
  - When award is conferred: Notify recipient and broadcast (`module: 'rewards'`, `priority: 'normal'`, `link: '/rewards/awards'`).
  - When peer kudos is posted: Notify recipient (`module: 'rewards'`, `priority: 'normal'`, `link: '/rewards/kudos'`).

### 14. Feedback & Grievance (STRICT CONFIDENTIALITY) (`backend/src/modules/feedback/`)
- In `feedback.service.js`:
  - When confidential grievance is filed: Notify ONLY members of the Grievance Inquiry Committee and HR (`module: 'feedback'`, `priority: 'urgent'`, `link: '/feedback/admin'`).
  - When status updates: Notify strictly the filing employee (`module: 'feedback'`, `priority: 'high'`, `link: '/feedback/track'`).

### 15. Attrition Prediction (`backend/src/modules/attrition/`)
- In `attrition.service.js`:
  - When employee flight risk exceeds critical threshold (>75%): Notify SuperAdmin and HR Director (`module: 'attrition'`, `priority: 'urgent'`, `link: '/attrition'`). Strictly confidential.

### 16. System & Security (`backend/src/modules/auth/`)
- In `auth.service.js`:
  - When login from unrecognized device or new IP occurs: Notify user (`module: 'system'`, `priority: 'high'`, `link: '/login-history'`).

## Verification Criteria
- [ ] Every business workflow event triggers `notify()` with appropriate `module` and `priority`.
- [ ] Confidential events (Grievances, Attrition risks, Compensation) do not leak to unauthorized roles.
- [ ] Notifications contain actionable direct links to approval screens or relevant records.
