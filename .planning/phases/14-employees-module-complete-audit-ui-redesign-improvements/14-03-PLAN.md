# Plan 14-03: Employee Create & Edit Wizard Redesign (`EmployeeFormPage.jsx`) & Supporting Modals

## Goal
Completely redesign the 5-step employee onboarding and editing wizard (`frontend/src/features/employees/pages/EmployeeFormPage.jsx`) with the Forest & Emerald Enterprise theme, reactive dependent dropdowns, Pakistani CNIC formatting, and account provisioning. Modernize `StatusChangeModal.jsx` and `DocumentUploadModal.jsx`.

## Scope & Implementation Details

1. **`frontend/src/features/employees/pages/EmployeeFormPage.jsx` Redesign**:
   - Modernized visual step indicator (1. Personal Details, 2. Contact & Emergency, 3. Job Assignment, 4. Bank & Financials, 5. Account & Review).
   - High-contrast card surfaces: `bg-white dark:bg-[#111D17]` with 1px border `border-slate-200 dark:border-[#1A3125]`.
   - **Step 1: Personal Details**:
     - Auto-generated or custom Employee Code.
     - Full Name, Official Email, Phone Number.
     - Pakistani CNIC mask (`XXXXX-XXXXXXX-X`) with 13-digit NADRA validation and expiry date picker.
     - Date of Birth and Gender selection.
   - **Step 2: Contact & Emergency**:
     - Full residential address (Street, City, Province, Postal Code, Country).
     - Emergency contact person, relationship, and contact phone.
   - **Step 3: Job Assignment**:
     - Department selection dropdown.
     - Reactive Designation dropdown (filtered dynamically based on the selected Department).
     - Shift selection with work timings display.
     - Reporting Manager selection via `ManagerSelect.jsx`.
     - Employment Type (Permanent, Contract, Intern, Probation) and Joining Date.
     - Probation End Date (mandatory if status is Probation).
   - **Step 4: Bank & Financials**:
     - Bank Name, Account Title, Account Number, and IBAN (with Pakistani format validation).
   - **Step 5: Account & Review**:
     - Toggle to automatically create linked `User` login account (`createAccount`).
     - Role selection via `RoleSelect.jsx` (defaults to Employee role).
     - Full review summary before submission.
     - After creation: Account credentials dialog displaying temporary login credentials with copy-to-clipboard action.
   - **PBAC Guarding**:
     - User must possess `employee.create` (for new hires) or `employee.edit` (for existing records). Unauthorized access redirects to `/employees`.

2. **`StatusChangeModal.jsx` Modernization**:
   - Apply Forest & Emerald styling (`dark:bg-[#111D17]`, `dark:border-[#1A3125]`).
   - Status options: `Active`, `Probation`, `Notice`, `Resigned`, `Terminated`, `Suspended`.
   - Prominent danger alert banner when selecting critical statuses (`Terminated`, `Suspended`, `Resigned`) warning of automatic user account deactivation.
   - Mandatory transition justification reason textarea (minimum 5 characters).

3. **`DocumentUploadModal.jsx` Modernization**:
   - Full dark mode support.
   - Supported document categories: `CNIC Copy`, `Degree Certificate`, `Employment Contract`, `NDA`, `Experience Letter`, `Other`.
   - Client-side validation: Max 5MB, formats allowed: PDF, PNG, JPG, WEBP.
   - Clear loading spinner and success notifications.
