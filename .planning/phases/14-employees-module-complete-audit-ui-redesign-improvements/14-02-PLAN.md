# Plan 14-02: Employee Comprehensive Detail Modal & Profile View Modernization

## Goal
Implement a brand new, highly responsive `EmployeeDetailModal.jsx` allowing users to inspect full employee details directly from the directory without losing their active search, filters, or pagination state. Modernize `EmployeeProfilePage.jsx` and its sub-tabs (`ProfileOverviewTab.jsx`, `ProfileDocumentsTab.jsx`, `ProfileHistoryTab.jsx`, `ProfileSalaryTab.jsx`) to adhere to the Forest & Emerald Enterprise theme.

## Scope & Implementation Details

1. **`frontend/src/features/employees/components/EmployeeDetailModal.jsx` (New Component)**:
   - High-impact modal opened upon row-click or card-click in `EmployeeListPage.jsx`.
   - Organizes employee data into 10 structured sections/tabs:
     1. **Overview & Header**: Avatar, Name, Employee Code, Status Badge, Role, Department.
     2. **Personal Information**: DOB, Gender, Masked CNIC, CNIC Expiry alert.
     3. **Employment Details**: Employment Type, Status, Date of Joining, Probation Period.
     4. **Department & Designation**: Grade, Job Title, Department Code.
     5. **Reporting Structure**: Reporting Manager line and team scope.
     6. **Contact Coordinates**: Official Email, Phone, Residential Address, Emergency Contact.
     7. **Documents & Attachments**: Uploaded vault contracts, degrees, CNIC copies with download links.
     8. **Employment History**: Timeline of promotions, transfers, and title changes.
     9. **Account Information**: Linked User account status, 2FA status, last login timestamp.
     10. **Financial Overview**: Salary structure & bank coordinates (strictly masked if user lacks `employee.salary.view`).
   - Action Bar:
     - "Edit Profile" button (PBAC guarded: `employee.edit`).
     - "Change Status" button (PBAC guarded: `employee.edit`).
     - "Reset Password" button (PBAC guarded: `user.manage`).
     - "Archive Employee" button (PBAC guarded: `employee.delete`).
   - Clean handling of missing or undefined fields (renders '—' instead of null or empty strings).

2. **`EmployeeProfilePage.jsx` & Sub-Tabs Modernization**:
   - Update `EmployeeProfilePage.jsx` and its tab components:
     - `ProfileOverviewTab.jsx`: Dark theme tokens (`dark:bg-[#111D17]`, `dark:border-[#1A3125]`), CNIC and probation expiry countdown alerts.
     - `ProfileDocumentsTab.jsx`: Modern document list with type badges, upload date, file size, download link, and delete action.
     - `ProfileHistoryTab.jsx`: Chronological timeline of career transitions and promotions with emerald nodes.
     - `ProfileSalaryTab.jsx`: Financial compensation breakdown with PBAC guard and unmasked/masked values.
