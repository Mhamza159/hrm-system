## 2. File Level Changes

### 1. `backend/tests/attendance.wfh-face-notifications.e2e.test.js` (New Test Suite)
Create end-to-end integration tests using Vitest & Supertest covering:
- **Suite A: Face Enrollment & Face Punch Guard**
  1. Employee without enrollment attempts Face Punch $\rightarrow$ Rejected (400, `FACE_ENROLLMENT_REQUIRED`).
  2. No attendance record created in database.
  3. Employee with valid approved enrollment performs Face Punch $\rightarrow$ Succeeds (200, status `present`).
  4. Employee with rejected/pending enrollment $\rightarrow$ Rejected (400).
  5. Successful face enrollment submission $\rightarrow$ Creates notification for HR Managers and Super Admins.
  6. Biometric collision rejection $\rightarrow$ Does NOT create HR notification.
- **Suite B: WFH Advance-Notice Restriction**
  7. Employee applies for WFH 1 day in advance (tomorrow) $\rightarrow$ Allowed (201/200).
  8. Employee applies for WFH on same day (today) $\rightarrow$ Rejected (400, `WFH_ADVANCE_NOTICE_REQUIRED`).
  9. Employee applies for past date $\rightarrow$ Rejected (400).
  10. Employee applies 5 days in advance $\rightarrow$ Allowed (201/200).
- **Suite C: WFH Notifications & Approval Flow**
  11. WFH application creates notification for direct manager and HR managers.
  12. Notification contains correct employee name, date, and link (`/attendance/team?tab=wfh`).
  13. WFH decision creates notification for applicant with link (`/attendance/my?tab=wfh`).
