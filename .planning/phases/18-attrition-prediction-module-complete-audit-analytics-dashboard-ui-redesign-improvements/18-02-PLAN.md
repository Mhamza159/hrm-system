# Plan 18-02: Interactive Analytics Charts Suite (Risk Donut, Department Stacked Bars & Risk Factors Breakdown)

## 1. Goal
Audit, modernize, and build the **Interactive Analytics Charts Suite** for the Attrition Prediction Module. Deliver high-craft, responsive native SVG charts adhering to the Forest & Emerald Enterprise theme (`#111D17` surface, `#1A3125` borders) without adding external chart libraries (following Ponytail principles). Include a **Department Risk Stacked Bar Chart** (`DepartmentRiskChart.jsx`), an **Attrition Risk Distribution Donut Chart** (`AttritionRiskDonutChart.jsx`), and an **Attrition Contributing Factors Breakdown Chart** (`AttritionFactorsChart.jsx`) with interactive tooltips and click-to-filter drill-downs.

---

## 2. Target Files & Scope
- `frontend/src/features/attrition/components/DepartmentRiskChart.jsx`
- `frontend/src/features/attrition/components/AttritionRiskDonutChart.jsx` (New)
- `frontend/src/features/attrition/components/AttritionFactorsChart.jsx` (New)

---

## 3. Detailed Tasks

### Task 1: Department Risk Stacked Bar Chart (`DepartmentRiskChart.jsx`)
- Modernize the existing native SVG stacked bar chart:
  - Background: `#111D17` surface with `#1A3125` border and `#F8FAFC` headings.
  - X-Axis: Department names/codes.
  - Y-Axis: Employee headcount with horizontal grid lines (`#1A3125`).
  - Stacked Bar Segments:
    - High Risk: `#EF4444` (crimson)
    - Moderate Risk: `#F59E0B` (amber)
    - Low Risk: `#00C471` (emerald)
  - Interactive Hover Tooltip:
    - Floats over hovered bar showing department name, total headcount, average risk score, and exact counts/percentages for High, Moderate, and Low risk.
  - Click-to-Filter Drill-Down:
    - Clicking a department bar sets the active department filter on the employee table.

### Task 2: Attrition Risk Distribution Donut Chart (`AttritionRiskDonutChart.jsx`)
- Build a lightweight native SVG donut/ring chart:
  - Visual segments proportionally sized according to High, Moderate, and Low risk employee counts.
  - Center cutout displaying the overall workforce Average Risk Score with health classification (`Low Risk` / `Caution` / `Elevated Risk`).
  - Interactive Legend:
    - Colored indicators with employee count and percentage share.
    - Hovering a legend item highlights the corresponding ring segment.
    - Clicking a legend item filters the employee table by that risk tier.
  - Empty state with clean fallback when no scores are evaluated yet.

### Task 3: Contributing Factors Breakdown Chart (`AttritionFactorsChart.jsx`)
- Build a horizontal feature-importance / signal weight chart:
  - Visualizes the 5 core behavioral drivers used by the scoring engine:
    1. **Attendance & Tardiness Signals** (Weight: 30%)
    2. **Compensation & Increment Stagnation** (Weight: 25%)
    3. **Performance & Appraisal Trajectory** (Weight: 20%)
    4. **Tenure & Role Progression** (Weight: 15%)
    5. **Engagement & Workplace Signals** (Weight: 10%)
  - Progress bars with color coding reflecting average impact across evaluated employees.
  - Tooltips detailing the specific threshold triggers (e.g. `>3 absences = +15pts`, `>18 months without increment = +25pts`).
