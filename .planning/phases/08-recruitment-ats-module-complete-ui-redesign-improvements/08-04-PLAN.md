# Plan 08-04: Candidate Pipeline Board & Internal Mobility/Referral Pages Overhaul

## Objective
Redesign the ATS Candidate Pipeline Kanban board (`CandidatePipelinePage.jsx`), the Internal Jobs & Employee Referrals portal (`InternalJobsPage.jsx`), and all supporting candidate modals to create a unified, responsive, and production-quality recruitment workflow aligned with `DESIGN.md`.

---

## Proposed Changes

### 1. `frontend/src/features/recruitment/pages/CandidatePipelinePage.jsx`
- Redesign Kanban layout:
  - Filter bar: Job selector dropdown, search query input, and AI score threshold filter.
  - Column stages: `Applied`, `Shortlisted`, `Interview`, `Technical Test`, `Offer Issued`, `Hired` (with count badges and stage-specific color borders).
  - Candidate Cards:
    - Candidate name, email, applied date, source (Direct or Employee Referral).
    - `AIScreeningBadge.jsx` integration with match score pill and reasoning tooltip.
    - Quick stage-move arrows and action menu (Inspect, Schedule Interview, Submit Scorecard, Issue Offer, Hire, Reject).
- Row/card click opens `CandidateDetailModal.jsx`.

### 2. Candidate Modals Modernization
- `CandidateDetailModal.jsx`: Comprehensive drawer/modal with tabs for Overview, AI Analysis, Timeline History, and Actions (Reject, Advance Stage, Download CV).
- `InterviewScheduleModal.jsx`: Clean datetime picker, interviewer assignment, round type selector, and meeting link field.
- `ScorecardModal.jsx`: Structured rubric evaluation (Technical Skills, Cultural Fit, Communication) with numerical rating and feedback text.
- `OfferLetterModal.jsx`: Offer details (base salary, joining date, expiry date) with PDF download preview.

### 3. `frontend/src/features/recruitment/pages/InternalJobsPage.jsx`
- Modern internal jobs board:
  - Tab 1: **Open Positions (Internal)** — cards with department, salary, experience, and direct "Refer Candidate" or "Apply Internally" buttons.
  - Tab 2: **My Referrals** — high-density table of submitted referrals with candidate name, position, stage pill, referral reward indicator, and submission date.
- Redesign `EmployeeReferralModal.jsx`:
  - Position selector, referee name, email, phone, relationship, and resume upload.
