# Plan 39-02: Conversion of Core Management Drawers to Centered Modals

**Objective:** Convert the 3 core management drawers (`OrgChartDetailDrawer`, `PolicyDetailDrawer`, and `NotificationDrawer`) from right-docked slide-overs into responsive, horizontally and vertically centered modals.

---

## 1. Scope & Targeted Files

1. `frontend/src/features/departments/components/OrgChartDetailDrawer.jsx` (the exact component from the user's screenshot)
2. `frontend/src/features/policies/components/PolicyDetailDrawer.jsx`
3. `frontend/src/features/notifications/components/NotificationDrawer.jsx`

---

## 2. Requirements & Implementation Details

### A. `OrgChartDetailDrawer.jsx`
- **Current Issue:** Renders as `fixed inset-0 ... flex justify-end` with a right-docked drawer `max-w-md md:max-w-lg h-full border-l slide-in-from-right` and dark backdrop `bg-slate-950/60 dark:bg-black/75`.
- **Target Architecture:**
  - Wrap in `fixed inset-0 z-50 flex items-center justify-center p-4 sm:p-6 bg-slate-900/40 backdrop-blur-sm animate-in fade-in duration-200`.
  - Convert container to `relative w-full max-w-xl max-h-[88vh] flex flex-col rounded-2xl bg-white dark:bg-[#111D17] border border-slate-200 dark:border-[#1A3125] shadow-2xl overflow-hidden animate-in zoom-in-95 duration-200`.
  - Maintain all functional capabilities:
    - Center on Canvas button & Focus Hierarchy button.
    - Direct reporting manager card with instant click-to-navigate.
    - Ancestral reporting chain breadcrumbs.
    - Direct reports roster with click-to-navigate.
    - PBAC coordinate masking.
    - Inspect Full Employee Record button.

### B. `PolicyDetailDrawer.jsx`
- **Current Issue:** Uses `fixed inset-y-0 right-0 max-w-full flex pl-10` with a slide-over sheet `w-screen max-w-2xl h-full border-l` and `bg-slate-900/60`.
- **Target Architecture:**
  - Convert to centered modal: `max-w-3xl max-h-[90vh] rounded-2xl border border-slate-200 dark:border-[#1A3125] shadow-2xl`.
  - Retain policy metadata, version history, acknowledgement tracking, attachment downloads, and action triggers.

### C. `NotificationDrawer.jsx`
- **Current Issue:** Uses `absolute inset-y-0 right-0 max-w-full flex pl-10` with `w-screen max-w-md h-full border-l` and `bg-black/60`.
- **Target Architecture:**
  - Convert to centered modal dialog: `max-w-lg max-h-[85vh] rounded-2xl border border-slate-200 dark:border-[#1A3125] shadow-2xl`.
  - Retain live filter tabs (All, Unread, Urgent), "Mark all read", "Archive all", real-time items list, and "View full notifications page" navigation.

---

## 3. Verification Criteria

- [ ] `OrgChartDetailDrawer` opens cleanly centered in the screen with subtle backdrop and rounded corners.
- [ ] `PolicyDetailDrawer` renders as a centered document inspection modal.
- [ ] `NotificationDrawer` renders as a centered notification feed modal.
- [ ] Internal scrolling functions smoothly without full page scrolling issues.
- [ ] All interactive buttons and callbacks function without regression.
