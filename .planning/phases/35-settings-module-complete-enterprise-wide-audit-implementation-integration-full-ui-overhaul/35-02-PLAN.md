# Plan 35-02: Company Profile, Logo Management, Direct Removal & System-Wide Branding Synchronization

**Phase**: 35 — Settings Module Complete Overhaul  
**Status**: Planned  
**Layer**: Fullstack (Backend Routes/Controllers + Frontend Component)  
**Estimated Complexity**: Moderate  

---

## 1. Goal
Overhaul Section 1 (Company Profile) to provide complete corporate identity management:
1. Support company logo upload (Cloudinary + Local storage fallback) with preview, cropping guidance, and format validation (PNG, JPEG, WEBP, SVG max 2MB).
2. Add dedicated backend endpoint and frontend action for **Logo Removal / Reset to Brandmark** (`DELETE /api/v1/settings/company/logo`).
3. Provide live "Brand Artifact Preview" showing how company name, NTN, STRN, address, and logo render on:
   - PDF Payslips
   - Employee ID Cards
   - Automated Email Header / Footer
4. Sync updated company logo to Topbar and Public Policies in real time.

---

## 2. Requirements & Scope

1. **Backend Endpoints (`backend/src/modules/settings/setting.routes.js`, `setting.controller.js`)**:
   - `DELETE /api/v1/settings/company/logo`: Clears `logoUrl` from `company` setting, invalidates cache, and records audit log. Restricted to `settings.manage`.
   - Update `POST /api/v1/settings/company/logo` to return updated company setting payload.

2. **Frontend Component (`frontend/src/features/settings/components/CompanyForm.jsx`)**:
   - Full redesign using Elvara Forest `#061A14`, Emerald `#00C471`, Card `#FFFFFF`/`#111D17`.
   - Legal Details Card: Legal Name, NTN, STRN, Registration/Tax Status badge, Industry, Established Year.
   - Contact & Address Card: Email, Official Phone, Website, Physical Address with copy action.
   - Logo Asset Studio: Drag-and-drop zone, current logo thumbnail with zoom preview, "Change Logo" and "Remove Logo" actions.
   - Interactive Preview Switcher: Live preview tabs for (1) Payslip Header, (2) Employee Badge, (3) Email Signature.
   - Unsaved changes indicator and form validation with helpful error states.

---

## 3. Implementation Steps

1. Add `deleteCompanyLogo` controller in `backend/src/modules/settings/setting.controller.js`.
2. Register `DELETE /company/logo` route in `backend/src/modules/settings/setting.routes.js`.
3. Add `useDeleteLogoMutation` in `frontend/src/features/settings/settingsApi.js`.
4. Overhaul `frontend/src/features/settings/components/CompanyForm.jsx` with the Elvara Forest styling and Brand Artifact Preview.
5. Verify logo removal and upload flow end-to-end.

---

## 4. Verification & Testing
