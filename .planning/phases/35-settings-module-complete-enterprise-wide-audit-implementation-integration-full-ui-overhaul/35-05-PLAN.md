# Plan 35-05: Payroll Cycle Integration, Pakistan FBR Progressive Tax Engine & Statutory Compliance Simulator

**Phase**: 35 — Settings Module Complete Overhaul  
**Status**: Planned  
**Layer**: Fullstack (Backend Service & Calculator + Frontend Forms)  
**Estimated Complexity**: High  

---

## 1. Goal
Overhaul Section 5 (Payroll Cycle) and Section 6 (Statutory Tax):
1. **Payroll Cycle**:
   - Cycle type ('calendar_month' vs 'custom_range'), cycle start day, end day, disbursement day.
   - Calculate attendance lock date (e.g. 2 days before cycle end) and salary generation trigger schedule.
   - Connect `payroll.service.js` to consume `SettingService.getSetting('payrollCycle')` instead of hardcoding calendar dates.
2. **Statutory Tax & Compliance**:
   - Pakistan FBR salaried income tax slabs editor with integer paisa precision, validation preventing bracket overlaps or gaps, and preset loader for FBR Tax Years (2024-2025, 2025-2026).
   - EOBI compliance (5% employer, 1% employee, minimum wage 37,000 PKR).
   - PESSI provincial social security (6% employer rate, wage ceiling in PKR/paisa).
   - Provident Fund (8.33% employer/employee rates).
   - **Interactive Tax & Net Salary Simulator**: Real-time calculator allowing admins to input any monthly gross salary (e.g., 150,000 PKR) and see annual tax, monthly withholding tax, EOBI employee/employer share, and net take-home salary computed live.

---

## 2. Requirements & Scope

1. **Backend Integration (`backend/src/modules/payroll/payroll.service.js`, `taxCalculator.js`)**:
   - Provide helper `getPayrollCycleBounds(month, year, payrollCycle)` that calculates `startDate`, `endDate`, `attendanceLockDate`, and `disbursementDate` based on configured settings.
   - Validate tax slab continuity in `backend/src/modules/settings/setting.validation.js` ensuring `minPaisa` equals previous slab `maxPaisa + 1`.

2. **Frontend Components**:
   - `frontend/src/features/settings/components/PayrollSettingsForm.jsx`:
     - Cycle type selector (Calendar month vs Custom span).
     - Start, end, and disbursement day inputs with auto-calculated calendar timeline preview.
     - Attendance lock window warning indicator.
   - `frontend/src/features/settings/components/TaxSlabsForm.jsx`:
     - Tax Year selector with "Load FBR 2024-2025 Defaults" and "Load FBR 2025-2026 Defaults" quick buttons.
     - Dynamic slab table with inline PKR formatting, rate % input, and fixed tax PKR input.
     - EOBI, PESSI, and Provident Fund cards with explanation tooltips.
     - **Interactive Live Simulator Widget**: Gross salary input with instant visual breakdown of Gross, FBR Monthly Tax, EOBI (PKR 370), Provident Fund, and Net Take-Home Pay.

## 3. Implementation Steps

1. Update `backend/src/modules/settings/setting.validation.js` to validate tax slab continuity and add preset helpers.

3. Redesign `frontend/src/features/settings/components/PayrollSettingsForm.jsx` with timeline visualization.
