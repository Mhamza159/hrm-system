# Plan 35-03: Working Hours, Shift Synchronization, Attendance Rules, Dynamic QR & Biometric Privacy Engine

**Phase**: 35 — Settings Module Complete Overhaul  
**Status**: Planned  
**Layer**: Fullstack (Backend Service Alignment + Frontend Components)  
**Estimated Complexity**: Moderate  

---

## 1. Goal
Overhaul Section 2 (Working Hours) and Section 3 (Attendance Rules):
1. **Working Hours**: Standard weekly operating days (7-day toggle with at least 1 required), standard shift timing (`startTime`, `endTime`), mandatory break duration, net working hours calculator, canonical `Asia/Karachi` timezone badge, and alignment notes with Module M4 Work Shifts.
2. **Attendance Rules**:
   - Late arrival grace period (`graceMinutes`: 0–60m), late threshold minutes, half-day late count threshold, and late deduction penalty in PKR / integer paisa.
   - **Dynamic QR Attendance Kiosk Governance**: Master enable/disable toggle, rotation interval selection (15s, 30s, 45s, 60s), anti-replay TTL window, hardware device binding governance.
   - **Facial Recognition Attendance Governance**: Master biometric enable/disable toggle, Euclidean distance confidence threshold slider (0.30 to 0.70, default 0.50), anti-spoofing Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) liveness requirement toggle, and biometric privacy data protection disclaimer (local vector storage, no third-party cloud data transmission).

---

## 2. Requirements & Scope

1. **Working Hours (`frontend/src/features/settings/components/WorkingHoursForm.jsx`)**:
   - Visual 7-day pill selector with active indicators.
   - Office timing inputs (HH:mm format) with auto-calculated total shift duration and net working hours.
   - Half-day threshold duration in minutes/hours.
   - Flexible shift allowance toggle.
   - Timezone notice: Explicitly displays `Asia/Karachi (UTC+5:00)`.

2. **Attendance Rules (`frontend/src/features/settings/components/AttendanceRulesForm.jsx`)**:
   - Card 1: Punctuality & Grace Periods (Grace minutes, Late threshold, Half-day threshold, Penalty per late deduction with PKR conversion).
   - Card 2: Dynamic QR Kiosk Attendance (Global toggle, rotating token interval 15s/30s/45s/60s, hardware device binding status).
   - Card 3: Biometric Face Recognition (Global toggle, Euclidean match threshold slider with strict/relaxed tolerance hints, liveness detection toggle, biometric privacy compliance badge).
   - Card 4: Simulation sandbox showing how a punch at 09:16 is treated under current grace rules.
