## 3. Detailed Tasks

### Task 1: Dedicated Employee Policy Portal (`EmployeePoliciesPage.jsx`)
- Employee-centric interface displaying published policies scoped to their department and role:
  - **Banner**: "Employee Policy Portal & Handbooks" with acknowledgment status summary ("2 policies require your mandatory signature").
  - **Tabs**:
    - **Action Required / Pending Sign-off**: Highlights policies requiring acknowledgement that the employee has not yet signed. Shows countdown of remaining grace period days.
    - **Acknowledged Policies**: Historical archive of policies signed by the employee with date/time of signature.
    - **All Company Policies**: General directory of company regulations and guidelines.
  - **Policy Cards**:
    - Clear sign-off CTA button ("Review & Acknowledge" in pulsing emerald).
    - Status chip ("Pending Your Signature" vs "Signed on 12 Jan 2026").
    - Category tag, reading time estimate, and document attachment badge.

### Task 2: Digital Policy Acknowledgement Modal (`PolicyAcknowledgementModal.jsx`)
- Legally compliant acknowledgement signing flow:
  - Header: Policy title, version number (`v2.1`), and category.
  - Mandatory reading section: Employee must scroll through policy text or confirm having read attached document.
  - Legal declaration checkbox:
    - *"I confirm that I have read, understood, and agree to abide by the rules and principles stated in this policy."*
  - Digital signature input: Employee types their legal full name to confirm identity.
  - Metadata captured: Automatically records client timestamp, IP address, and browser user-agent.
  - Submit action: Calls `POST /api/v1/policies/:id/acknowledge`. Upon success, displays green verification checkmark and updates portal state immediately.

### Task 3: Compliance & Acknowledgement Tracking Sheet (`PolicyAcknowledgementRosterModal.jsx`)
- Comprehensive administrative compliance tracker (`policy.track`):
  - Fetches `/api/v1/policies/:id/acknowledgements`.
  - **Compliance Progress Gauge**:
    - Total eligible staff, total signed, pending count, completion percentage (`82% Compliant`).
  - **Department-wise Breakdown**:
    - Progress bars per department (e.g. `Engineering: 18/20 (90%)`, `Sales: 12/15 (80%)`, `Finance: 10/10 (100%)`).
  - **Employee Roster Table**:
    - Columns: Employee Name, Department, Designation, Status (`Signed` with timestamp vs `Pending`), Signature Text, IP Address.
  - **Actions**:
    - Search by employee name or code.
    - Filter by department and signature status.
    - "Export Compliance CSV" button downloading clean spreadsheet for compliance audits.
    - "Remind Pending Employees" button dispatching batch in-app reminder notifications.

### Task 4: Routing & Navigation Updates
- In `frontend/src/App.jsx`:
  - Route `/policies`: Accessible to all authenticated users with `policy.view`.
    - If user is Super Admin or HR Manager: default view is `PoliciesDashboardPage.jsx` with tab/link to employee view.
    - If user is regular Employee: default view is `EmployeePoliciesPage.jsx`.
- In `frontend/src/components/layout/Sidebar.jsx`:
  - Update `Company Policies` link to point to `/policies` guarded by `policy.view`.
