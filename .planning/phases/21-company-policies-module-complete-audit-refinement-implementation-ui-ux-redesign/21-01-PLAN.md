## 3. Detailed Tasks

### Task 1: Mongoose Models Implementation
- **`Policy`**:
  - Fields: `title`, `policyCode`, `categoryId`, `description`, `content`, `fileUrl`, `fileType`, `fileSize`, `version`, `status` (`Draft`, `Pending Approval`, `Approved`, `Published`, `Rejected`, `Archived`, `Expired`), `effectiveDate`, `reviewDate`, `expiryDate`, `ownerDepartmentId`, `ownerId`, `scope` (`All Employees`, `Department Specific`, `Role Specific`, `Management Only`), `applicableDepartmentIds`, `applicableRoleNames`, `requiresAcknowledgement`, `acknowledgementGracePeriodDays`, `approvedBy`, `approvedAt`, `publishedAt`, `rejectionReason`, `isDeleted`.
  - Indexes: Text index on `title`, `description`, `content` for AI/search queries. Indexes on `status`, `categoryId`, `isDeleted`.
- **`PolicyCategory`**:
  - Fields: `name`, `code`, `description`, `icon`, `color`, `displayOrder`, `isActive`, `isDeleted`.
- **`PolicyVersion`**:
  - Immutable historical record storing previous version snapshots, `policyId`, `version`, `title`, `content`, `fileUrl`, `changeSummary`, `effectiveDate`, `publishedAt`, `createdBy`, `approvedBy`.
- **`PolicyAcknowledgement`**:
  - Tracking employee sign-offs: `policyId`, `policyVersion`, `employeeId`, `userId`, `signatureText`, `acknowledgedAt`, `ipAddress`, `userAgent`, `isLatestVersion`.
  - Compound unique index: `{ policyId: 1, policyVersion: 1, employeeId: 1 }`.

### Task 2: Permissions Catalogue & Role Seeding
- In `backend/src/shared/permissions.js`:
  - Register subject: `Policy`.
  - Actions:
    - `policy.view`: View published policies applicable to the user.
    - `policy.create`: Author new policy draft.
    - `policy.edit`: Edit policy draft.

    - `policy.approve`: Approve or reject submitted policy proposals.

### Task 4: Service Layer & Business Logic (`policy.service.js`)
- **Lifecycle & State Machine**:
  - `Draft` $\rightarrow$ `Pending Approval` $\rightarrow$ `Approved` $\rightarrow$ `Published` / `Rejected`.
  - `Published` $\rightarrow$ `Archived` / `Expired`.
  - Modifying a published policy creates a new pending version draft rather than mutating published records.
- **Scoping & Visibility**:
  - Filter policies by employee's department and role when accessed by regular employees.
  - HR Manager & Super Admin can view all statuses.
- **Acknowledgement Engine**:
  - Calculate acknowledgement completion percentage: $\left(\frac{\text{Acknowledged}}{\text{Total Required}}\right) \times 100$.
  - Record acknowledgement receipt with IP and user-agent binding.
  - Mark historical acknowledgements as `isLatestVersion: false` when a new revision is published.
- **Notifications Hook**:
  - Dispatch in-app alert via `notify()` when a policy is published with acknowledgement requirement or approved/rejected.
- **Dashboard KPIs**:
  - Compute total, published, draft, pending approval, archived, requiring acknowledgement counts.

### Task 5: Controller, Routes & Integration Tests
- In `backend/src/modules/policies/policy.controller.js` and `policy.routes.js`:
  - Implement endpoints:
    - `GET /api/v1/policies`: List policies with filters & scoping.
    - `GET /api/v1/policies/stats`: Executive summary KPIs.
    - `GET /api/v1/policies/categories`: List categories.
    - `POST /api/v1/policies/categories`: Create category (`policy.manage`).
    - `GET /api/v1/policies/:id`: Get policy details.
    - `POST /api/v1/policies`: Create draft (`policy.create`).
    - `PUT /api/v1/policies/:id`: Edit policy (`policy.edit`).
    - `DELETE /api/v1/policies/:id`: Archive/delete (`policy.delete`).
    - `POST /api/v1/policies/:id/submit`: Submit for approval.
    - `POST /api/v1/policies/:id/approve`: Approve policy (`policy.approve`).
    - `POST /api/v1/policies/:id/reject`: Reject policy with reason (`policy.approve`).
    - `POST /api/v1/policies/:id/publish`: Publish policy (`policy.publish`).
    - `POST /api/v1/policies/:id/acknowledge`: Submit employee sign-off (`policy.acknowledge`).
    - `GET /api/v1/policies/:id/acknowledgements`: Compliance roster (`policy.track`).
    - `GET /api/v1/policies/:id/versions`: Version history (`policy.view`).
- In `backend/tests/policies.test.js`:
  - Verify complete lifecycle: Draft $\rightarrow$ Submit $\rightarrow$ Approve $\rightarrow$ Publish.
  - Verify employee acknowledgement and duplicate rejection.
  - Verify unauthorized role blocks (403).
