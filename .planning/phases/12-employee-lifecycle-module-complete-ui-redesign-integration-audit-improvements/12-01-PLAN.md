# Plan 12-01: Checklist Templates & Authoring Redesign

## Goal
Redesign the Checklist Templates master management interface (`ChecklistTemplatesPage.jsx`), create a read-only template rubric inspection modal (`ChecklistTemplateDetailModal.jsx`), and overhaul the authoring modal (`ChecklistTemplateModal.jsx`) to adhere to the Forest & Emerald Enterprise Craft, with strict task validation, department scoping, and safe modification warnings.

## Scope & Changes

1. **`frontend/src/features/onboarding/components/ChecklistTemplateDetailModal.jsx` (New Component)**:
   - Read-only inspection modal when clicking a checklist template card or row.
   - Displays template name, lifecycle stage (`onboarding` vs `offboarding`), department scope, and active/inactive status pill.
   - Lists all configured tasks in ordered sequence with category badge (`HR`, `IT`, `Department`, `Finance`), assigned role (`Employee`, `Manager`, `IT Admin`, `HR`), due offset days, and mandatory status indicator.
   - Includes quick action to edit (if user has `onboard.manage` permission).

2. **`frontend/src/features/onboarding/components/ChecklistTemplateModal.jsx` (Redesign)**:
   - Replace generic inputs with Forest & Emerald styling (`bg-white dark:bg-[#111D17]`, `border-slate-200 dark:border-[#1A3125]`).
   - Dynamic task builder:
     - Task Title & Description.
     - Category selector (`hr`, `it`, `department`, `finance`) with distinct visual icons and badges.
     - Role Assignee dropdown (`employee`, `manager`, `it_admin`, `hr`).
     - Due Days Offset (e.g. Day 0, Day 1, Day 3).
     - Mandatory toggle checkbox.
   - Safe Modification Notice: Warning banner explaining that edits affect future instantiated workflows without breaking historical records.
   - Validation ensuring at least one task is configured before submission.

3. **`frontend/src/features/onboarding/pages/ChecklistTemplatesPage.jsx` (Redesign)**:
   - Remove legacy `indigo-*` classes.
   - Top executive metric summary cards (Total Templates, Onboarding Templates, Offboarding Templates, Active Rubrics).
   - Dual lifecycle stage tabs (`Onboarding Templates` vs `Offboarding Templates`).
   - Department filter dropdown, search input, and table/card view options.
   - Clean action buttons (Create Template, View Rubric, Edit, Delete with accessible confirmation).
