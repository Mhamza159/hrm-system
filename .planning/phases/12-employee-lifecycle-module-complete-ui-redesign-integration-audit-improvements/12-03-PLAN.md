# Plan 12-03: Resignation & Notice Period Management Redesign

## Goal
Redesign the Resignation management portal (`ResignationPage.jsx`), create a dedicated resignation review and decision modal (`ResignationDetailModal.jsx`), and provide distinct self-service and management approval views with notice period tracking and confidential exit interview integration.

## Scope & Changes

1. **`frontend/src/features/onboarding/components/ResignationDetailModal.jsx` (New Component)**:
   - Comprehensive review modal opened when clicking a resignation record in the approvals queue.
   - Employee summary: Name, employee code, department, designation, joining date, and tenure calculation.
   - Resignation details: Submission date, intended last working day, primary reason (e.g. Better Opportunity, Relocation), and handoff/transition comments.
   - Decision controls for Manager / HR:
     - Accept Resignation: Set official Last Working Day (with notice period days calculation).
     - Reject Resignation: Mandatory rejection reason field.
     - Decision notes / handover expectations.
   - Exit Interview Tab (Confidential):
     - Displays submitted exit interview questionnaire feedback (reason for leaving, management rating, work environment feedback).
     - Strictly protected: Visible only to HR Manager and Super Admin (`offboard.manage`). Blocked for Department Managers.

2. **`frontend/src/features/onboarding/pages/ResignationPage.jsx` (Redesign)**:
   - Eliminate legacy `indigo-*` classes and adopt Forest & Emerald design system.
   - Dual-tabbed view:
     - **Tab 1: My Resignation (Self-Service)**:
       - Submission form for employees to initiate formal resignation with intended LWD, reason dropdown, and transition notes.
       - Active resignation status tracker: Displays current status (`submitted`, `accepted`, `rejected`), notice period countdown, official LWD, and link to complete the confidential exit interview.
     - **Tab 2: Approvals Queue / Team Resignations (Manager & HR)**:
       - Metric widgets: Pending Approvals, Under Notice Period, Accepted, Rejected.
       - Data table with search, department filters, status filters, intended vs official LWD, and review action button.
       - Clicking a resignation opens `ResignationDetailModal.jsx`.
